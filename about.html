Background & Philosophy

About Me

I am a Computer Science student and software developer based in Kathmandu, Nepal. I spend my time designing and building software systems with an emphasis on real-time streaming architectures, AI pipelines, computer vision models, and practical utility.

My journey as a builder is guided by curiosity and craftsmanship. Rather than assembling fragile surface-level demos, I focus on understanding underlying protocols—how WebSocket frames are streamed under latency constraints, how landmark coordinate vectors translate into accessible sign recognition, and how software design choices impact maintainability over years.

When I'm not writing Python or designing user interfaces, I explore spatial game architectures, geospatial analysis tools, and open-source contributions.
